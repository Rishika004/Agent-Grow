# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Founders, engineers, job seekers, and recruiters in the Indian startup ecosystem and beyond. They are active on LinkedIn but struggle to write posts consistently — they have the ideas, the experience, and the stories, but not the time or writing habit. They use LinkedIn to build credibility, get noticed, and grow their network. They are on the go, slightly impatient, and want results fast.

## Product Purpose

LNKD is an agentic AI tool that generates audience-targeted LinkedIn posts from three input types: a GitHub link, an image, or plain text experience. A 5-node AI pipeline (intake, enrichment, RAG, writer, critic) produces 3 scored post variants. Users pick, edit inline, and publish directly to LinkedIn or copy to clipboard. The product exists because writing good LinkedIn posts consistently is hard — LNKD removes the blank-page problem entirely.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, confident, creative. The product does serious work but doesn't feel corporate. It feels like having a brilliant ghostwriter who also understands tech, startups, and the Indian professional context. Tone: direct, no fluff, gets to the point fast.

## Anti-references

- Generic "AI writing tool" SaaS pages with stock photo heroes and three equal feature cards
- Jasper, Copy.ai — too wordy, too bloated, overwhelming UI
- Canva — too playful and consumer-y for a professional tool
- Purple AI gradients and mesh backgrounds — instant trust-killer
- Anything that looks like it was built from a Tailwind template

## Design Principles

1. **Speed over ceremony** — every interaction should feel instant. No loading spinners where skeletons work. No unnecessary steps.
2. **Confidence through restraint** — fewer elements, more impact. A sharp interface communicates that the product is sharp.
3. **Show the output, not the process** — the 3 post variants are the hero. Everything else is secondary.
4. **One decision at a time** — input type, then audience, then tone, then generate. No overwhelming the user.
5. **Professional but not boring** — dark, crisp, with a single electric accent. The UI should feel like a tool a founder would be proud to use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Reduced motion support for all animations. High contrast text throughout. Keyboard navigable. Works on mobile (founders check LinkedIn on phones).
